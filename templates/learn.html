How to take a useful photo

  • Use soft daylight; avoid harsh glare and deep shadow.
  • Hold steady and focus on one whole leaf.
  • Take a second photo showing the whole plant for your human advisor.
  • Avoid filters, screenshots and heavy editing.

Interpreting the score

A raw model score is not a calibrated probability of correctness. A high score cannot rule out a wrong match. The app’s threshold is a heuristic and is not field-calibrated.

Safe next step: Compare multiple leaves and the whole plant, then consult a qualified local agricultural advisor. Do not make pesticide decisions from this screen alone.
